Congratulations on taking the initial steps in becoming a digital humanist! To wrap up this tutorial, we will briefly discuss the benefits of digital methods for scholars and their research. Digital humanities research affords scholars opportunities that traditional humanities research cannot offer. Here is a list of some of the advantages that undertaking digital methods can offer your research and teaching.
 
- **New approaches to old questions** As we modeled in the earlier activities, digital methods allow researchers to revisit completed research projects and reconceptualize them by using new digital tools. 
- **Ability to ask new research questions**: The inclusion of digital tools in your skillset allows you to think about projects through a new lens and allows you to ask questions you didn’t think were possible with traditional research methods. 
- **Facilities collaboration**: Traditional humanities research tends to be individualistic. Scholars are rarely encouraged to partner with colleagues to carry out a research project. Digital humanities, on the other hand, maintains collaboration with others as a central pillar. One approach is to partner with someone whose skillset targets areas that make you uneasy. 
- **Develops digital literacy skills**: In the classroom, instructors are encouraged to help students develop critical skills for their careers. In our current digital age, students need to know how to navigate digital aspects in the workplace. Incorporating digital methods training in your course allows students to develop these skills and prepares them for their careers. 
- **Encourages active learning in the classroom**: Using digital tools requires that students become active learners in their education. They must engage with course content in ways that reading and lectures cannot accomplish. Think back how you created a hand-drawn map from a primary source. 
- **Fosters community engagement**: The idea with digital humanities projects is to make research accessible to people beyond academia. This helps bridge interactions between the university and the general public.   
- **Process large volumes of research**: How long would it take you to read an author’s entire collection? Digital tools can process an entire collection within seconds. Although you still need a person to interpret a tool’s output, the ability to remove your own bias can help you discover new patterns.
