Now that we have read the transcript of Lucille Cowley Nixon’s oral history, let’s take a moment to think about how we could use this primary source in a research project. For this next step, let’s write down 1-2 research questions or research project ideas that we could use with this primary source. These ideas do not have to be complex.

What did you come up with? Are your questions more involved than the sample ones provided above? The idea here is to get us to think about how we typically approach research, so that later in this tutorial, we can build on it by discussing what digital methods can offer us in our research.
