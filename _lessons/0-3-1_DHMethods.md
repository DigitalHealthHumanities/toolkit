In this tutorial, you have been asked to think about how you typically begin a research project as a way to remind us of the process that may be automatic. The idea here is to see how we can apply the approaches we have used for some time to take on a new challenge: conducting digital humanities research. Embarking on this journey can sometimes be cumbersome, especially if you feel uneasy with using digital tools. Grounding new adventures in processes we are familiar with helps lessen the uneasiness and, hopefully, allows us to start and/or continue working in new research disciplines. 

In this section of the tutorial, we would like you to think about your own research projects and to consider how you might apply digital methods to your research. We will break down the different sections to help you think through your options. 

Digital methods go beyond just mapping. There are several subfields within digital humanities, ranging from textual analysis to cultural heritage preservation and recovery. Below you can see some different areas of research in digital humanities work.
