## Step 1: The research process 

Let’s begin by outlining the steps we typically follow when starting a new project. Think about how you formulate your hypothesis or develop your research questions. Write down your process on a sheet of paper.

Had you considered how we use the scientific methods in humanities research? If you hadn’t, that is okay because we often connect the scientific method to research in science disciplines. However, if we compare our process for starting a new project, we can see how the steps we take can align with those in the scientific method. It’s possible that for many people, they haven’t thought about the scientific method in quite some time, even though they have been applying it in their research. We learned about this approach so long ago that it has become ingrained in our process, and we may not even recognize that we are using it. If you ever get stuck on how to begin a new research project, especially something in a field that is unfamiliar to you, go back to basics and think about the scientific method.
