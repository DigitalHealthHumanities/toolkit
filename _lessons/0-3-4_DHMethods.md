In the previous section, we asked you to outline some research questions that you have previously investigated. Now we will revisit those questions, with an eye to applying digital methods. 

Take 1-2 questions from the section above and answer the following questions. You may want to consider using a research question that uses the object you evaluated in Part 2 of this lesson. 

1. What method did you use to answer this research question? 
2. What objects/data did you analyze for this project? 
3. Take a look at the Methods Matrix again, what is one digital method you can use to answer a question like this?
4. In applying a digital method to this project, what do you imagine your final, published research findings will look like?
5. Are there any points of friction between your research object, your research method, and your final published research that you can see at this time? How might you address these concerns?
    
    
**CONGRATULATIONS!** You have taken the first steps towards designing a digitally-inflected research project.
