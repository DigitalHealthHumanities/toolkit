Let’s go back to the research questions that we drafted in Part 4 of this lesson. Here are sample questions: 
1. Who are the established families in South Central Texas at the turn of the twentieth century?
2. What was life like for Texas families at the turn of the twentieth century?
3. What are the migration patterns of Texas families at the turn of the twentieth century? 

Now that you have experienced how some digital tools can alter how we approach and analyze our data. What changes would you make to your existing research questions? Or what additional research questions come to mind that you had not considered previously?
