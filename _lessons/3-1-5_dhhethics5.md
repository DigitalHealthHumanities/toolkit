**This current lesson is an outline. There are elements that still need to be completed/fleshed out.**

You can start to incorporate ethics at any stage of your project. Ideally, you’ve started before you even conceptualized the project fully, and before you got funding or institutional approval to create it. However, it doesn’t always work like that. If you’ve found these lessons partway through your process, you can still use this moment—after reflecting upon and learning about your audience, yourself, power differentials, and you audiences’ strengths and needs—to re-design your project. 

What aspects of the work you are undertaking are the most supportive of your audience? 

Community concerns may suggest that a key element of your project–for example, you might have wanted to map incidents within a particular neighborhood–would be harmful to community members. As frustrating as this can be, it’s also an opportunity to reimagine your work, and consider whether there is another way to approach it.
